# PRD: Carried collectible cards across Stories, Places and Moments

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | Draft for review |

| Market | Bangladesh first, English and Bangla |
| Decides | **D1** which categories Carried covers · **D2** the single physical product every category sells through |
| Source | Product grilling session, 16 Nov 2026 (14 questions, answers recorded in the Decision log below) |

This document fixes two business decisions and the requirements that follow from them. It deliberately does **not** treat the current site's UI, routes or card editions as constraints. Those exist to serve this model and can be redesigned.

---

## 1. Problem statement

Carried today does one thing: a reader picks a book, writes the line it left in them, and shares a designed card. That is a good free hook, but it is a narrow paying market. Books are only one of the things people want to keep. People also want to hold on to a film, a trip, a parent, a first date, and they want to give those memories to someone else as an object.

The earlier business research for Bangladesh found two hard facts:

1. A single paper card around ৳150 cannot pay for its own acquisition. Facebook cost per purchase runs ৳350–1,500, so the business only works with larger orders and mostly organic customers.
2. The real buying moments are gifts: Valentine's Day, Eid, birthdays, graduations and Ekushey Boi Mela.

The shop concept also spreads one design across five unrelated objects (print, bookmark, tote, notebook, mug). Five objects mean five specs, five vendors and no signature product. A collector can't build a set out of them.

If we don't solve this, Carried stays a books-only toy whose physical side never pays for itself.

## 2. The two decisions

### D1: Categories

Carried covers **three categories** at launch.

| Category | Covers | Subject source | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stories** | Books, films, natok/drama, web series | Curated BD-first list **plus** write-your-own | Humayun Ahmed's *Himu*, *Aynabaji*, *Hawa*, a favourite series |
| **Places** | Travel spots, cities, hometowns, campuses, home places | Curated BD-first list **plus** write-your-own | Sajek, Cox's Bazar, Sreemangal, Old Dhaka, "my nanabari" |
| **Moments** | A person, a relationship, a day | Always written by the buyer | "Ammu", "our first date", "the wedding", "the friend who moved to Toronto" |

**Why these three:**

- Stories and Places are **shared subjects**. Many people write about the same film or the same hill town, so they keep the viral loop alive: "What did Sajek leave in *you*?" Collectors mostly live here.
- Moments are **personal subjects**. No one else writes about your Ammu, so they add little to the loop, but they are the strongest gift category and they line up with the gift calendar.
- Curated lists give browsing, search and shared subject pages. Write-your-own means no buyer is ever blocked by a gap in the list.

**Out at launch: Music and live events.** The fan culture is real (Coke Studio Bangla, concerts), but lines written about songs read very close to lyrics, which carries the highest copyright exposure. We'll revisit once a rights position exists.

### D2: The single product, the Carried card

Every category sells through **one physical object**: a premium, personalised, trading-style card. Think Pokémon or UNO in the hand, but bigger, heavier and posher. Anything else we sell is a wrapper around this card (a box, a sleeve, a frame), never a separate product line.

| Attribute | Decision |
|---|---|
| Format | Trading-card style, sold singly or in stacks |
| Sizes | **Pokémon 63 × 88 mm** and **Tarot 70 × 120 mm** (both kept for now; see open question Q-1) |
| Stock | **Black-core card stock** (the casino/trading-card material, about 310–330 gsm, opaque core, snap) with a linen or air-cushion finish |
| Premium piece | **Acrylic hero card**: any card can be made in thick acrylic as a display piece |
| Launch finishes | **Foil** and **acrylic** |
| Later finishes | Pressed (deboss/letterpress-style) cards. Rarity mechanics are explicitly not in the first launch |
| Content | **Words lead**: the line, subject, category, date, place and signer. **Photo is an optional paid add-on**, mainly for Moments |
| Production | **Every card is personal and printed to order.** No pre-made or curated decks, no stock risk |
| Order size | **Any count from 1.** Per-card price drops at 5, 10, 20 and 30+. Delivery charged on top |
| What makes a collection | **One Carried frame**, a **category mark** (Story / Place / Moment) with a colour cue in a fixed position, and the **owner's running number** ("No. 07 · carried by Rimi") |

**Why one card and not a merch range:** one spec, one vendor relationship, one QC checklist and one die. A book card, a Sajek card and an "Ammu" card all come out as the same object, so they stack, file and display as one set. That consistency is what turns personal cards into a collection.

**Why black-core stock with an acrylic hero rather than all acrylic:** acrylic is 2–3 mm thick, so a 30-card stack would be 6–9 cm tall, heavy and chip-prone in courier. Card stock keeps stacks light and shuffle-able. The acrylic piece gives the shelf-worthy, posh moment for the cards that matter most.

## 3. Customers

All four buyer types buy the **same card**. They differ only in why and how many.

| Buyer | Why they buy | Typical order | Launch focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gift-givers** | A keepsake for a partner, friend or parent on a fixed date | 1–10 cards, often with acrylic and a photo | **Yes** |
| **Collectors** | Keep what each book, film or place left in them; add cards over time | Small, repeat batches | **Yes** |
| Event and bulk buyers | Weddings, birthdays, clubs, schools, corporate gifting for their own guests or users | 20–100+ cards | After launch |
| Businesses (B2B) | Cafés, bookshops, cinemas, tourism spots reselling or giving cards | Repeat accounts | After launch |

Launch marketing targets **gift-givers and collectors** only. Event, bulk and B2B buyers can still order through the same flow; they just aren't the first push.

## 4. Goals

Targets are hypotheses built from the Bangladesh research. They get re-baselined after the first 50 orders.

| # | Goal | Type | Success | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Orders are large enough to survive acquisition cost | Business | Average order value ≥ ৳600 | ≥ ৳900 |
| G2 | Most buyers arrive without paid ads | Business | Blended CAC ≤ ৳150; ≥ 60% of orders from organic or shared-card traffic | ≥ 75% organic |
| G3 | Collectors come back | User / business | ≥ 25% of buyers place a second order within 90 days | ≥ 35% |
| G4 | The new categories pull their weight | User | ≥ 40% of printed cards are Places or Moments by month 3 | ≥ 55% |
| G5 | The product survives delivery and COD in BD | Business | Reprint/damage ≤ 3%; return-to-origin ≤ 15% | RTO ≤ 10% |

## 5. Non-goals

| Non-goal | Why |
|---|---|
| Curated or pre-made decks (e.g. "the Humayun Ahmed deck") | Every printed card is the buyer's own. That avoids stock risk and avoids needing consent for other people's lines |
| Music and live-event category | Lyrics-adjacent copyright exposure; revisit with a rights position |
| Rarity, packs or randomised finishes | Deferred past first launch; base collectibility comes from frame, marks and numbering |
| Pressed or debossed cards | Future finish once foil and acrylic production is stable |
| A wide merch range (mugs, totes, notebooks, bookmarks) | Conflicts with the single-product decision. The current concepts get retired, not extended |
| Global or USD launch | Bangladesh first; diaspora and international are later expansions |
| Selling access to the digital card | Digital creation and sharing stay free. That free card is the acquisition engine |

## 6. User stories

### Gift-giver

- As a gift-giver, I want to write a card about a person or a shared moment so that I can give something that only they would understand.
- As a gift-giver, I want to add our photo to a Moment card so that the gift feels personal at a glance.
- As a gift-giver, I want to upgrade the most important card to acrylic so that it can stand on their desk.
- As a gift-giver with a deadline, I want to see the delivery date before I pay so that the card arrives before Valentine's Day or Eid.
- As a gift-giver, I want to order just one card so that a small gift doesn't force a big bundle.

### Collector

- As a collector, I want to write a card each time a book, film or trip stays with me so that my collection builds up over time without me printing every time.
- As a collector, I want every card to carry my running number so that my stack reads as one collection.
- As a collector, I want to pick a film or place from a list and find it fast so that I'm not retyping titles and spellings.
- As a collector, I want to print a batch of cards I've written whenever I'm ready, at a lower per-card price for bigger batches, so that printing in bulk is worth it.
- As a collector whose film or place isn't listed, I want to type it myself so that I'm never blocked.

### Event and bulk buyer (supported, not marketed at launch)

- As an event organiser, I want to order 50 cards in one go at the best tier so that I can give one to every guest.

### Edge cases

- A buyer uploads a low-resolution photo: they are warned before paying and can continue or replace it.
- A subject in Bangla and English spelling variants (e.g. "Sajek" / "সাজেক") resolves to one curated subject.
- A typed subject duplicates a curated one: suggest the curated match, but never force it.
- A buyer reaches checkout with zero cards selected: the order can't be placed.
- The line overflows a 63 × 88 mm card that fits on 70 × 120 mm: tell the buyer before payment, never shrink the type silently.

## 7. Requirements

### P0: cannot launch without

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| P0-1 | **Three categories** with a category field on every card | Given a buyer creating a card, when they choose a category, then only Stories, Places or Moments are allowed, and the category is stored with the card and shown as its mark |
| P0-2 | **Curated subject lists** for Stories and Places, BD-first, bilingual | Lists are searchable in English and Bangla. At launch each has at least 100 subjects (books carry over from the existing catalogue; films, natok and places are new) |
| P0-3 | **Write-your-own subject** in every category; Moments are always written | A typed subject is accepted, length-capped and validated server-side like any other user text |
| P0-4 | **One card frame** used by every category, with the category mark and colour cue in a fixed position | A Story, a Place and a Moment card printed side by side share frame, type and layout; only the mark, colour cue and content change |
| P0-5 | **Owner running number** on every printed card | Numbers increase per owner across orders and never repeat for that owner |
| P0-6 | **Two sizes**: 63 × 88 mm and 70 × 120 mm | The buyer sees which size their line fits before paying; print files are produced at 300 dpi, CMYK-safe, with 3 mm bleed for both sizes |
| P0-7 | **Black-core stock base** with foil Carried stamp | A signed physical reference proof from the vendor exists for each size before launch |
| P0-8 | **Acrylic upgrade** for any card | Each card in an order can independently be set to acrylic; the price shows per card |
| P0-9 | **Optional photo add-on** | A photo is never required. When added, a minimum resolution is checked and the buyer is warned below it |
| P0-10 | **Any-count ordering with price tiers** at 1, 5, 10, 20 and 30+ | Per-card price at each tier shows before checkout; delivery is a separate line |
| P0-11 | **Print-to-order pipeline** | Each order exports print-ready files batched onto sheets; nothing is pre-printed |
| P0-12 | **Real ordering with personal data kept separate** | A new ADR replaces today's "no orders, no addresses" rule. Order data (name, phone, address) lives apart from lines and shelves, and addresses are deleted after the return window |
| P0-13 | **Payment and delivery fit for BD** | bKash prepaid and COD both work. Outside Dhaka, COD takes the delivery fee in advance. Couriers: Steadfast primary, Pathao backup |
| P0-14 | **Free digital card stays free** | Writing, saving and sharing any card in any category costs nothing and never needs an order |

### P1: fast follows

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| P1-1 | Gift box and sleeve wrappers | Offered as order add-ons; they contain cards and never replace them |
| P1-2 | Collection view of everything a person has written, marked printed or not printed | A collector can select unprinted cards and send them to one order |
| P1-3 | Shared subject pages for curated Stories and Places | Each curated subject shows public cards about it and invites a new one |
| P1-4 | Delivery-date promise on gift dates | Shows a "order by" date before Valentine's Day, Eid and Boi Mela |
| P1-5 | Bulk ordering path for events | One order of 20+ cards with per-guest lines, at the 30+ tier |

### P2: design for, don't build

| ID | Consideration | Why it shapes v1 |
|---|---|---|
| P2-1 | Pressed/debossed finish | Keep the finish a per-card attribute so a third value slots in |
| P2-2 | Rarity and special editions | Keep the running number and card metadata so rarity can attach later |
| P2-3 | Music and live-event category | Keep categories as data, not hard-coded layouts |
| P2-4 | Contributor cards (guests each write one) | Keep "who wrote it" separate from "who ordered it" |
| P2-5 | Diaspora orders (card payments, abroad shipping) | Keep currency and shipping region as order fields, not assumptions |
| P2-6 | B2B reseller accounts | Wholesale pricing stays a tier table, not special-case code |

## 8. Pricing structure

The **structure** is decided. The **numbers** are not, because black-core trading-card stock and acrylic have not been quoted yet. The earlier research priced paper A6 cards; those figures are only a starting reference.

| Item | How it's priced | Reference from earlier research |
|---|---|---|
| Card, per unit | Per-card price falling at 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30+ | Paper A6 card: cost ৳45–60, price ৳160–190 |
| Acrylic upgrade | Per card, on top | Not yet quoted |
| Foil upgrade | Per card or included in base (see Q-3) | Foil hit about ৳9 per card |
| Photo add-on | Flat per card | Working figure ৳50–100 |
| Delivery | Separate line | ৳70 inside Dhaka, ৳120–130 outside |
| Pricing rule | Price = cost ÷ (1 − margin), targeting 60–70% gross margin | Expect 15–30% net if ads drive most sales, 35–45% if mostly organic |

## 9. Success metrics

**Leading (weekly from launch):** orders, AOV, cards per order, acrylic and photo attach rate, share of cards by category, organic vs paid order share, checkout completion, days from order to dispatch.

**Lagging (monthly):** 90-day repeat-order rate, blended CAC, gross margin %, return-to-origin %, reprint rate, month-6 order count against the 300 orders/month research scenario.

Evaluate at 2 weeks after soft launch, after Boi Mela and Valentine's Day (end of Feb 2027), and at month 6.

## 10. Distribution

1. **Every free card is an ad.** Shared cards carry the Carried mark and lead back to "write your own".
2. **Ekushey Boi Mela and Valentine's Day, Feb 2027.** The two biggest gift and Stories moments land in the same month.
3. **Book, film and travel communities** in Bangladesh: reading groups, film pages, travel groups. Gift 20–30 creators a set instead of buying ads.
4. **Retargeting ads only**, aimed at people who already made a free card.

## 11. Timeline and phasing

Today is 16 Nov 2026. Boi Mela and Valentine's Day are the hard deadline.

| Phase | When | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Prove the object | Late Nov – mid Dec 2026 | Black-core and acrylic samples from at least 3 Dhaka vendors, both sizes; one vendor plus backup chosen; foil die made |
| 1. Build | Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 | Categories, curated lists, one frame, ordering ADR, order flow, print-file export |
| 2. Soft launch | Jan 2027 | 50 orders from friends, reading and travel groups; fix QC; re-baseline goals and prices |
| 3. Gift-season push | Feb 2027 | Boi Mela and Valentine's Day campaign; creator gifting; retargeting only |
| 4. Review | Mar 2027 | Real cost, RTO, CAC, repeat rate; reprice; decide on bulk, B2B and the next finish |

**Dependencies:** a vendor who can print black-core stock and acrylic one card at a time (blocks Phase 1 pricing), a trade licence and bKash merchant account (blocks Phase 2), and the ordering ADR (blocks taking real orders).
