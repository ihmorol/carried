# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers who want to express and share the one line a book left in them, in English or Bangla. No account is required.

## Product Purpose

Pick a book, write an original short reflection, sign it, and create a designed Carried card. The card and link invite another reader to write their own.

## Positioning

A library catalog card the reader gets to author. URL-carried payloads preserve the line and reader-added book metadata without requiring a persistent server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Digital creation, Paper/Midnight/Moss inks, downloads, and sharing are free. My lines lives on the device. Optional shared feeds publish the submitted line and name; they are not a private shelf or account system. Do not add tracking, authentication, payment collection, or order/address storage.

A launch-ready merchandise concept storefront lets readers personalize physical product concepts and keep a local cart. Ordering is not open. Prices, production specifications, commercial rights, fulfillment, payment provider, shipping, and policies remain launch decisions. Do not invent those facts.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Carried's name, wordmark, understated literary voice, library-card artwork, accession number, ownership stamp, three inks, and stamp-red accent. The approved migration uses the user's reading-room reference for digital composition and media-dashboard reference for the storefront composition, not its movie content, gradients, accounts, or glass.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing curated books and editorial reflection corpus in src/lib/books.ts and src/lib/seeds/. Existing local font files and actual DOM/canvas/OG card renderers. No approved production product photography or live merchandise catalog is supplied. Book-cover access does not grant merchandise reproduction rights.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve English and Bangla support, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, readable contrast, and device-local fallback when browser storage is unavailable.
